01HOW WE MET

An ordinary day, apparently

The stranger.

I had just started my SQI then. That day, the bikeman who took me to school didn't have change after I paid him the amount he requested.

I was just stuck there with the bikeman, because no one wanted to leave their money. I honestly didn't know how long I was going to stay outside.

Then you happened to pass by. It was my first time seeing you. You stopped and paid the bikeman for me, and I was really grateful because I would have stayed there for so long.

I asked for your class and name so I could return the money. And honestly… you were really cute and calm. I liked you instantly! 😭

I didn't think much of you because I didn't expect us to become close after that.

I thought it would be a one-day thing: return the money, say thank you, and that would be all. Life clearly had other plans.
